# ABC itinerary comparison

Checked 25 November 2026. Each entry describes one publisher’s schedule. A walking day has a scheduled trail stage. A mixed day includes a trail stage and a drive on the same date. Walking-only days exclude mixed days. Pokhara totals begin and end in Pokhara. They exclude travel between Pokhara and Kathmandu or an international airport.

The four operator itineraries are attributed planning references. Their schedules establish what the publisher describes. Actual journey times, personal suitability and current road access require separate checks. The Siwai track is drawn from the frozen KeiTreks evidence. Operator distance bands from the distance guide have no verified one-to-one mapping to these schedules. The comparison uses short factual stage summaries; no open licence is asserted for the operators’ page text, maps or photographs.
